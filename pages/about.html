About StudentHub

One place for everyday academic life.

StudentHub helps students find notices, organise deadlines and stay connected to campus opportunities.

Our purpose

Clear information, less friction.

The portal brings fragmented academic updates into a focused, accessible experience. It is designed to work just as well on a phone between lectures as it does on a laptop.

  • Academic progress and attendance at a glance
  • Current notices and important deadlines
  • Events, workshops and campus opportunities
  • Secure account-based access as the backend is configured
