Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for using StudentHub. Contact support if you still need help.

How do I create a StudentHub account?

Open Register, enter your details and use a strong password. The page confirms success only after the configured server accepts the account.

Why is my dashboard marked demonstration data?

The visible academic records are samples until the authenticated PHP API and institution database are connected.

How do I register for an event?

Choose an event and use “Ask to register.” A secure event-registration endpoint can replace this after the backend is configured.

I cannot sign in. What should I do?

Check your email and password, then contact the portal support team if the problem continues.
